How this works

Each part is measured from its audio: spectrum in 24 bands from 30 Hz to 16 kHz, left and right balance per band, stereo width (side vs mid), level while playing, and how sharp its hits are. Crowding adds every part's energy over the pan and pitch grid, spread by its width. A clash is two parts that play at the same time, sit close in pan, share at least a quarter of their spectrum and are within 12 dB. Open spots are the emptiest areas between 60 Hz and 12 kHz, away from the hard-panned edges. Suggestions come from your Sound Galaxy: sounds whose brightness and width fit the spot. See METHODS.md.
